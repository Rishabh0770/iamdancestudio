About Sanju - Professional Dancer & Choreographer

I Am Dance Studio is a well-known and trusted dance academy based in Solan, dedicated to shaping passionate dancers from an early age. Founded by a professionally trained dancer with years of experience, our academy has been inspiring students to express themselves through the art of dance since childhood.

With a strong foundation built on discipline, creativity, and confidence, we provide high-quality dance training for children, teenagers, and adults. Our mission is to nurture talent, boost self-confidence, and help every student discover their true potential.

At I Am Dance Studio, we believe that dance is not just a skill — it is a way of life. Our experienced instructors focus on personal attention, modern teaching techniques, and a friendly learning environment to ensure every student grows both technically and personally.
