Meet Your Instructor – Sanju

I Am Dance Studio is a premier dance academy established with a vision to nurture talent and foster a deep passion for dance. Under the expert guidance of Sanju, our founder and principal instructor, we have trained over 1000+ students across multiple disciplines.
